A Practical, Non-Speculative Completion Plan

Complete named story lines

The ten public character achievements explicitly require all major commissions for that person. Treat each as a character checklist, not as one commission.

Track pigeon commissions

“Birds of a Feather, Frock Together” is separate from the named-character achievements and requires every commission received via pigeon.

Reserve challenge dresses

The accessory, trim, and spending achievements specify one dress. Plan each attempt around its visible threshold and ensure the expensive dress is successful.

Do not chase guessed hidden triggers

Progress naturally, check Steam after major story or crafting milestones, and wait for verified evidence before treating a title pun as an unlock instruction.

FAQ

How many Dressmaker achievements are there?

There are 32 on Steam: 16 with public conditions and 16 whose conditions are hidden on the official page.

Are the hidden achievement names spoilers?

Some names may hint at story beats. This page shows the names Steam makes public but does not reveal or guess the hidden criteria.

Are global unlock percentages requirements?

No. Percentages show how many tracked players have unlocked an achievement; they are not part of its condition and can change over time.
