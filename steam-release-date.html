Dressmaker Steam Release Date: September 21, 2026

Dressmaker is scheduled to launch on Steam on September 21, 2026. Steam may display September 22 in time zones where the global unlock falls after midnight, including China. At the last check, the full game was still locked; the free itch.io Prototype 0.5.5 remained playable while players waited.

Quick Answer

Dressmaker is scheduled for September 21, 2026 globally and may appear as September 22 in your local Steam client. The developer's September announcement lists a US price of $14.99, regional pricing, and a 10% launch discount. Check the store before paying because the displayed local price and availability are controlled by Steam.

What To Do Around Launch

  1. Open the official Steam page to see whether the game has unlocked in your region and to confirm your local price.
  2. Play the free prototype if you want to learn the cutting, sewing, fabric, and customer request loop before the full game arrives.
  3. Avoid APK and reupload sites. The current official playable build is a PC prototype on itch.io, not an Android APK.
  4. Check your platform. Steam currently shows requirements for Windows and macOS. The free itch.io prototype also has a Linux build.

What The Steam Version Adds

  • A longer commission campaign: the price announcement describes approximately 35 hours of story commissions, excluding off-the-rack dresses.
  • A much larger design library: the same announcement lists more than 150 dress pieces, 450 fabrics, and 350 accessories.
  • A bigger town setup: the Steam description focuses on townsfolk with individual tastes and important events.
  • More shop progression: the full version describes reputation, coin, and selling pre-made dresses from your shop window.
  • More creative control: the store page highlights fabric choices, pattern arrangement, sewing, buttons, bows, applique, and lace trims.
  • Steam requirements: the current store page shows Windows and macOS requirements; check the listing again for any platform updates.

FAQ

What is the Dressmaker Steam release date?

The global Steam date is September 21, 2026. Depending on your time zone, Steam may show September 22. Check the store page for the live unlock status.

Can I download Dressmaker now?

Yes. The free itch.io prototype is available now for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Use the official download page instead of third-party APK or EXE mirrors.

Will Dressmaker have Chinese language support?

The Steam page currently lists English, Simplified Chinese, and Japanese interface, audio, and subtitle support.

Is Dressmaker on mobile?

No official Android or iOS version is available right now. Treat APK download pages as unsafe unless the developers announce an official mobile build.
