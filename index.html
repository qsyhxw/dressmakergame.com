About the Game

Dressmaker blends mindful crafting with playful problem-solving. Sketch ideas in your notebook, pick fabrics in the shop, arrange pattern pieces to minimize waste, then sew neat seams and fit garments on the mannequin before sending customers off in style. As a dressmaking sim and sewing game, it rewards creativity and clean stitches.

FAQ

Is Dressmaker free?

Yes. It's a name-your-price prototype on itch.io—support the devs if you enjoy it!

Which platforms are supported?

The full release is confirmed for Windows and macOS. The separate itch.io Prototype 0.5.5 has Windows, macOS, and Linux builds.

Will there be a full release?

Yes. Dressmaker released on Steam on September 21, 2026 globally and is available to buy now.

How do I fix the Mac "malicious software" warning?

Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll down → click "Open Anyway". See our detailed Mac installation guide.

What's the difference between itch.io and Steam versions?

The itch.io download is the smaller, name-your-price Prototype 0.5.5. Steam is the separate complete commercial release with a larger town, campaign, and design library. See the sourced comparison.

How do I get 100% quality score when sewing?

Follow the dotted line precisely, slow down at corners, and align pattern pieces to the grain line (arrows pointing left/right). Full tutorial: Sewing Mastery Guide.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Once downloaded, Dressmaker runs entirely offline with no internet connection required.

Is there a mobile version (Android/iOS)?

No official Android or iOS release has been announced. The full release is confirmed for Windows and macOS; the prototype also has a Linux build.

How long is the prototype?

The prototype is a smaller slice with limited customers and dress patterns. For the full release, the official price announcement describes approximately 35 hours of story commissions, excluding off-the-rack dresses.

Where can I get help or report bugs?

Join the official Discord community ↗ or check our troubleshooting guide.
